Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

F1 News Publisher is an independent tool for creating short Formula 1 news videos and preparing them for publication on TikTok.

Information We Collect

When users authorize TikTok integration, the service may process TikTok account identifiers, authorization tokens, and information necessary to upload videos.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to connect authorized TikTok accounts, prepare video uploads, and publish content as approved by the account owner.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide the requested integration.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account information and access tokens against unauthorized access.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account information and authorization tokens are retained only as needed for the integration. Users may request deletion of their information or revoke access through TikTok account settings.

Third-Party Services

Use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own privacy policy and terms.

Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact the project operator through the contact information published on the official project website.
