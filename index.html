F1 News Publisher

Formula 1 news, racing highlights and short-form video content.

About the Project

F1 News Publisher is a web-based tool for preparing short Formula 1 videos using racing news, commentary and media authorized for use.

How It Works

The tool collects relevant racing news, prepares scripts and voiceovers, and assembles short videos. Authorized users can review content before sharing it to their connected TikTok accounts.

TikTok Integration

The planned TikTok integration uses authorized account access to submit videos through TikTok's official API. Publishing features are subject to TikTok's approval.
